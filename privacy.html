Privacy Policy

Window Gang Business Report

Effective: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CAM Hunter Service, Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") handles information accessed through the Window Gang Business Report, an internal business management application.

Purpose of the Application

Window Gang Business Report is an internal application used by authorized personnel for business administration, financial reporting, expense review, and related operational functions.

QuickBooks Online Information

The application may connect to QuickBooks Online through Intuit's authorized OAuth process. When authorized, the application may access accounting information necessary for its internal business functions, including transactions, accounts, vendors, expense categories, and related financial information.

QuickBooks Online credentials and passwords are not collected by the application. Authentication is handled through Intuit's authorization services.

How Information Is Used

Information accessed through QuickBooks Online is used only for legitimate internal business purposes, including financial analysis, transaction review, accounting administration, reporting, and maintaining business records.

Information Sharing

Information obtained through the application is not sold, rented, or used for advertising. Information is not disclosed to third parties except when necessary to operate the business, comply with applicable law, or use authorized service providers supporting business operations.

Data Access and Security

Access to the application and its financial information is limited to authorized users. Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect business information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Data Retention

Business information is retained only as necessary for legitimate business, accounting, operational, and legal purposes.

Disconnecting QuickBooks

Authorization for the application to access QuickBooks Online may be revoked or disconnected. After authorization is revoked, the application will no longer be able to obtain new QuickBooks Online information using that authorization.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the application or its use changes. Updates will be reflected on this page.
